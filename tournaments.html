Competition Structure

Tournament format

1
Two Groups
The 8 registered teams are divided into two groups of four teams.
2
Group Stage
Each team competes inside its group. Results determine the group ranking.
3
Knockout Stage
The qualified teams advance to the semifinals, followed by the final and third-place match.

Podium & Awards

Player Information

Important rules

  • Each registration is for one fixed team of two players.
  • Teams must arrive on time and be ready before their announced match.
  • Players must bring suitable padel shoes, rackets and personal equipment.
  • Match scoring and exact match duration will be explained before the tournament starts.
  • The organizer may adjust the schedule when required to keep the event running smoothly.
  • Respect toward opponents, staff and tournament decisions is required at all times.
